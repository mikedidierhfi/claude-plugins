# Phase 08 — QA before delivery

Run the full checklist in [reference/sanity_checks.md](../reference/sanity_checks.md). In short:

1. **Open the workbook in Excel once.** Confirm derived formulas compute (no `#REF!`/`#VALUE!`).
   CIQ cells populate (CapIQ add-in present) or show `#NAME?` (expected if not — note the fallback).
2. **Tie-outs:** TEV > 0 and sensible; market cap ≈ shares × price vs a known quote; EBITDA ≥ EBIT;
   multiples in believable ranges; `nm` shown for non-positive denominators.
3. **Flags reviewed:** every ⚠ in the footnotes is acceptable or addressed (stale items, financials,
   dual-class, missing add-backs). No silent errors.
4. **Sources complete:** filings (accession/period/URL), price (source+timestamp), consensus
   (provider+date) all present.
5. **Cross-check (Phase 06):** computed clean figures vs the IR deck; adjusted-vs-clean gaps noted.

Deliver with a 2–3 sentence summary: what's compared, the headline multiples, the methodology in one
line, and **explicitly** which names are caveated (financials) and whether NTM populated (CapIQ add-in)
or needs the user's login. If anything couldn't be computed, say so plainly.
